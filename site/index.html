Listening

  1. Hold fn and speak. Dots appear and move more as your voice gets louder.
  2. Transcribed on your Mac. Parakeet V3 turns speech into text, offline.
  3. Sent for you. The text is pasted and Return is pressed.

How it works

  1. fn

    Hold fn

    Recording starts and the island appears. Prefer hands-free? Tap fn twice to start, once to stop.

  2. Speak

    Talk normally. The ring around the send button shows the time used; recordings stop on their own at two minutes.

  3. Return

    Release

    Parakeet transcribes on your Mac, the text is pasted into the frontmost app, and Return is pressed for you if Auto-Enter is on.

Press esc to cancel. Toggle Auto-Enter from the menu bar with ⇧⌘E.

What you get

On device

NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 runs locally through FluidAudio on CoreML and the Neural Engine. Audio never leaves the Mac.

8 MB app

A native SwiftUI app of about 8 MB. It lives in the menu bar and stays idle until you hold fn.

Auto-Enter for coding agents

Optionally press Return after pasting, so a spoken prompt goes straight to Claude Code, T3 Code or ChatGPT.

Works in any app

Text is pasted into whatever is in front: editors, terminals, browsers, mail, notes.

Open source

MIT licensed. Read the code, build it with make app, or send a pull request.

Light and dark

The island and settings window follow the system appearance, with the same thermal accent in both.

Try it on your Mac

Needs macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon, plus Microphone and Accessibility permission. The build is not notarized: the first time, right-click the app and choose Open.
